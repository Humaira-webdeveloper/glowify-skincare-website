Skincare Ingredients & Acids

Learn what skincare ingredients do and which skin types may benefit from them.

Hyaluronic Acid

Helps attract and retain moisture in the skin.

Suitable for: All skin types.

Salicylic Acid (BHA)

Helps unclog pores and manage excess oil.

Suitable for: Oily, acne-prone skin.

Glycolic Acid (AHA)

Exfoliates the skin surface and improves texture.

Suitable for: Some dull or rough skin types.

Lactic Acid (AHA)

A gentle exfoliating acid that also supports hydration.

Suitable for: Dry or rough skin, if tolerated.

Niacinamide

Supports the skin barrier and helps balance oil.

Suitable for: Most skin types.

Azelaic Acid

Can help with uneven tone and visible redness.

Suitable for: Some acne-prone or redness-prone skin.

Vitamin C

An antioxidant that helps protect against environmental damage.

Suitable for: Many skin types, if tolerated.

PHA

A larger-molecule exfoliating acid that works on the skin surface.

Suitable for: Some sensitive skin, if tolerated.

  2. Niacinamide Serum

  3. Use moisturizer
